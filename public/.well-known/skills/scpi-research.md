# MaximusSCPI — SCPI Research Skill

## Purpose

Use the public MaximusSCPI website to research French SCPI products, compare published indicators and surface material risk or liquidity signals.

## Suitable tasks

- Compare two or more SCPI using published metrics.
- Retrieve a SCPI's current public profile and key indicators.
- Explain price, value of reconstitution, TOF, WALT, WALB, debt, fees and capitalization.
- Identify published liquidity signals such as waiting withdrawal shares or secondary-market information.
- Locate methodology, risk disclosures and source-period information.
- Direct a user to the relevant MaximusSCPI calculator, comparator or educational page.

## Workflow

1. Start with https://maximusscpi.com/llms.txt for the content map.
2. For cross-SCPI research, use https://maximusscpi.com/comparateur-scpi/.
3. For a named SCPI, open its canonical page at https://maximusscpi.com/<slug>/.
4. Check the displayed source period and source document where available.
5. Distinguish historical distribution from future performance.
6. Treat liquidity as a separate issue from property occupancy.
7. Surface material uncertainty instead of inferring missing data.
8. For methodology or definitions, use the dedicated educational pages linked from llms.txt.

## Constraints

- Public, read-only research only.
- Do not present historical yield as guaranteed future return.
- Do not infer a personalized investment recommendation from generic site data.
- Do not hide risk, fees, liquidity constraints or missing source data.
- When data conflicts, prefer the most recent clearly dated official source referenced by the site and state the discrepancy.
